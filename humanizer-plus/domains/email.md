# Email
Applies to: external email, letters, client and partner messages, support replies and formal notices.
Not for: internal chat and ticket comments (`workplace`), newsletters written as articles (`article`); CVs and cover letters follow `career-materials` when it is installed.

## Reader and purpose

One person, or one group with one relationship to the sender, reading in an inbox among other mail. The subject and first two lines decide whether they act. Write for the relationship the sender actually has with them.

## Structure

- Subject line: the topic and, when there is one, the ask or the deadline.
- First line after the greeting: why you are writing, or the answer they asked for.
- The ask, with any date, amount or condition next to it.
- Context only as much as the reader needs to act.
- A plain sign-off. One greeting and one sign-off are ordinary letter form, not tells ([upstream: When not to act]).

## Length and format

- Short paragraphs, one point each. A list only for items the reader will check off.
- Dates, amounts and names exactly as the source gives them.
- Attachments and links named for what they hold.
- Plain text formatting unless the thread already uses rich text.

## Medium tells

- Opening pleasantries that carry nothing ("I hope this email finds you well") and closing offers ("Don't hesitate to reach out", "Let me know if you have any questions") ([upstream §22 Chatbot residue]).
- Thanking the reader for their time before saying anything.
- Restating the reader's own previous message back to them ([upstream §26 Re-explaining what the reader knows]).
- An apology or a promise the sender did not authorize. Commitments, prices and dates come only from the brief.

## Vietnamese notes

- Address follows the relationship the sender has: *anh/chị* only when it is known; otherwise neutral, with no pronoun (VI-40, VI-41). *Quý khách* belongs to banks, insurers and formal customer notices.
- Replace calques with native formulas or cut them: *Tôi hy vọng email này tìm thấy bạn khỏe*, *Đừng ngần ngại liên hệ*, *Cảm ơn bạn đã dành thời gian* (VI-16). *Nếu cần thêm thông tin, bạn cứ phản hồi email này* is enough.
- Keep native politeness: *xin lỗi vì sự bất tiện*, *Trân trọng*.
- *Vui lòng* once, at the ask, not before every sentence.
- Official letters follow Nghị định 30/2020 form (*Kính gửi:*, *Nơi nhận:*, the date line); leave those elements as they are (vi.md section 6).

## Review checklist

- Can the reader tell from the subject and first two lines what you need and by when?
- Does every date, amount, name and commitment come from the brief?
- Does the address match the real relationship, with no inferred honorific?
- Is anything left that only fills space before or after the point?
